# Business Questions

This project uses SQL to answer key business questions from the Northwind database.

## Customer Analysis

1. Who are the top 10 customers by total spending?
2. Which customers purchase the widest variety of products?

## Product Analysis

3. Which products generate the highest revenue?
4. What is the total quantity sold for each product?
5. Which product is the best seller in each category?

## Category Analysis

6. Which product categories generate the most revenue?

## Supplier Analysis

7. Which suppliers contribute the highest sales revenue?
8. Which suppliers have products across the most categories?

## Employee Analysis

9. Which employees processed the highest-value orders?

## Sales Trend Analysis

10. What are the monthly sales trends?

## Future Analysis

As the project grows, additional analyses may include:
- Year-over-year sales growth
- Customer retention analysis
- Average order value
- Revenue by country
- Top-performing shippers
